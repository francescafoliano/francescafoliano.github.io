I am an education and labour economist based in London and Stockholm. My current research covers gender equality in the labour market, the geographical and social mobility of young adults, working from home and labour market outcomes, and the effects of school choice and competition in local education markets.

**Positions and affiliations**

- Assistant Professor in Economics, University of Southampton
- Research Associate, Economic Statistics Centre of Excellence (ESCoE)
- Research Associate, Stockholm School of Economics
- Visiting Fellow, Department of Social Policy, London School of Economics
- Member, Society of Economics of the Household

**Research interests:** economics of education; labour economics; wellbeing; international trade.

**Contact:** f.foliano@soton.ac.uk
